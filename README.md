This is the legend of Chronicles of the Fallen King, the story hidden behind the arena where you fight: Centuries ago, the kingdom flourished under the protection of the Four Relics of the Sun. The Great King not only ruled justly but was also the primary guardian of the balance between light and the Abyss. However, in his ambition to forever protect his people from death, he made the ultimate mistake: he opened the gates of the underworld to absorb immortality. Dark magic did not save him; it corrupted him. His body was deformed with obsidian horns, and his heart became burning coal. He betrayed his knights, devastated the cities he once protected, and proclaimed himself the Demon King. To prevent anyone from sealing him away again, he divided the sacred lands into endless halls guarded by his minions and entrenched himself in the heart of the infernal cauldron.
